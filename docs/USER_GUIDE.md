# User Guide: Mastering the Jambubrowser

Jambubrowser is a high-performance sovereign OS designed for elite researchers and privacy advocates.

## 💎 The Hybrid Interface
Jambubrowser features a unique **Split-View** architecture:
- **Left Sidebar**: Your Agentic Partner. Chat, research, and command the swarm.
- **Right Workspace**: A full-featured web browser with multi-tab support.
- **Floating Command Bar**: Center-bottom pill for rapid-fire research and autonomous commands.

## 📍 Deep Trust Navigation
Jambubrowser bridges the gap between AI claims and original evidence:
- **Interactive Citations**: Click any `[1]` or source chip in the chat to instantly navigate the browser to that source.
- **Source Peeking**: Hover over citations to see a translucent summary of the source URL.

## 🐝 Swarm Research (God Mode)
Activate **🔥 FULL POWER** to unleash the swarm:
- **Parallel Missions**: The agent breaks your query into 3 sub-tasks and researches them simultaneously.
- **Council Debate**: The 'Optimist' and 'Skeptic' personas audit findings in real-time to eliminate AI hallucinations.

## 🌟 The Workspace Canvas
Move beyond simple chat. Use the **📋 Workspace** tab to build a visual evidence board:
- **Pinning**: Click the 📌 icon next to any fact in the chat to save it to your board.
- **Organization**: Rearrange your findings visually to spot connections.
- **Drafting**: Use the "🖋️ Generate Whitepaper" button to convert your entire board into a professional Markdown report autonomously.

## 🛡️ Stealth & Sovereignty
Control your digital footprint in the **Stealth** tab:
- **Tor Routing**: Route all agentic traffic through the Onion network for ultimate anonymity.
- **Incognito Mode**: Enable "Forensic Safety" to ensure no cookies or cache persist after a research round.
- **Anonymity Meter**: Watch your privacy score change in real-time as you toggle proxy rotation and fingerprint randomization.
- **Credential Vault**: Store local logins to allow the agent to autonomously research behind professional portals.

## 🪐 3D Satellite Brain
Explore your knowledge space in the **🛰️ Brain Graph** tab:
- **Interactivity**: Click nodes to see original facts and source URLs.
- **Semantic Linkage**: Glowing lines represent conceptual connections drawn by the agent.
- **Zoom/Rotate**: Use your mouse to fly through your growing local knowledge vault.

## 🔥 Full Power (God Mode)
Activate the **🔥 FULL POWER** toggle in the header to unleash the agent's maximum capabilities:
- **Deep Research**: 3 rounds of recursive investigation.
- **Multi-Agent Swarm**: Concurrent research threads.
- **Panoramic Vision**: High-resolution, full-page visual analysis.
- **High Temperature**: Increased analytical creativity and insight discovery.

## 📱 Mobile Access
Access your desktop research on the go:
- Open the **Stealth** tab and find the **Mobile Bridge** section.
- Scan the QR code with your phone.
- Read and search your Local Brain over your home Wi-Fi without cloud sync.
